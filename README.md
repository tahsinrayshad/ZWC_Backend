# ZeroWasteConnect – Community Food & Resource Sharing Platform

## Overview

ZeroWasteConnect is a community-driven platform that connects individuals and organizations with surplus resources to those in need, addressing the critical paradox of food insecurity and material waste coexisting in urban environments. The platform aims to reduce waste, feed the hungry, and build stronger communities while supporting environmental sustainability.

## Problem Statement

Globally, over 1.3 billion tons of food go to waste annually while nearly 828 million people face hunger. In densely populated cities like Dhaka, Bangladesh (23,000+ people per square kilometer), this problem is particularly acute:

- Dhaka generates 5,500–6,000 tons of solid waste daily
- 60% of waste is organic, including significant food waste
- Many families in urban slums face chronic food insecurity
- Usable items like clothes, electronics, and household goods are discarded while others lack access

## Mission & Vision

**Mission**: Create a technology-enabled platform that facilitates the redistribution of surplus resources from those who have them to those who need them.

**Vision**: Build a circular economy where communities unite to eliminate waste, reduce hunger, and combat climate change through responsible resource sharing.

## UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) Alignment

ZeroWasteConnect directly supports six UN SDGs:

- **SDG 2** – Zero Hunger: Redirecting surplus food to those in need
- **SDG 3** – Good Health & Well-being: Promoting safe food sharing practices
- **SDG 4** – Quality Education: Providing sustainability education and awareness
- **SDG 11** – Sustainable Cities & Communities: Fostering community engagement
- **SDG 12** – Responsible Consumption & Production: Encouraging reuse and sharing
- **SDG 13** – Climate Action: Reducing carbon footprint through waste reduction

## Key Features

### Core Functionalities
- **Item Listing**: Donors can post surplus items with details (expiry date, condition, location)
- **Discovery & Matching**: Location-based search and recommendation system
- **Request & Handover**: Secure communication and coordination system
- **Feedback & Rating**: Trust-building through user reviews and ratings
- **Impact Dashboard**: Visual tracking of meals donated, waste prevented, and CO₂ saved
- **Community Engagement**: Educational content about sustainability and food safety

### Special Features
- Waste Impact Tracker
- Community Leaderboard
- NGO Partnership Portal
- Geolocation-based matching
- Educational resource library

## User Types & Roles

| User Type | Description | Key Capabilities |
|-----------|-------------|------------------|
| **Donor** | Individuals or institutions offering items | Create listings, manage donations, rate recipients, view impact stats |
| **Recipient** | Individuals or households in need | Browse items, make requests, communicate with donors, provide feedback |
| **Admin** | System administrators and moderators | Verify NGOs, moderate content, monitor analytics, manage user roles |
| **NGO Partner** | Registered non-profit organizations | Bulk donations, manage recipient lists, coordinate distributions, run campaigns |

## Use Cases

### Primary Use Cases
1. **Surplus Food Donation**: Individuals can share excess home-cooked meals or leftover food
2. **Resource Sharing**: Donate books, clothes, electronics, and household items
3. **Community Education**: Access educational content about waste reduction and sustainability
4. **NGO Collaboration**: Partner organizations can facilitate large-scale distributions
5. **Impact Tracking**: Monitor personal and community-wide environmental impact

### Example Scenarios
- A family cooks extra food and shares it with nearby students
- Someone moving homes donates furniture and appliances to local families
- An NGO coordinates restaurant surplus distribution to homeless shelters
- Community members compete in waste reduction challenges
